# Loot Goblins v0.7.9 — Trade Table + My Goblin Tray

## Trade Table

Replaces the old one-way trade/give flow with a two-player trade table.

### New trade flow
- Start a trade with another player.
- Both players can add or remove cards from their own offer.
- Zero-card offers are allowed, so gifts/bribes are supported.
- Either player can tap Ready.
- Changing an offer resets both Ready/Confirm states.
- Once both players are Ready, both must Confirm Trade.
- Either player can Cancel.

### Privacy
- Trade participants see exact offered cards.
- Observers only see that two players are trading.
- Received cards use the private card-gain splash.

## My Goblin Tray

Adds a persistent "Your Goblin" strip to the hand tray.

Shows:
- name
- Calling/Kin
- Glory
- Power
- Gear count
- status/effects count

Tap it to open your full character/player detail panel.
